Planet Fling Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026 · Developer: Gelila Mamo

Planet Fling is a free arcade game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. The developer does not run any servers for the game and does not collect your personal information directly.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone only: best scores, coins, unlocked and equipped alien skins, your daily challenge streak, earned achievements, and your sound, music and vibration settings. This data is not sent to the developer. It is removed when you use Reset progress in Settings (which keeps your sound settings) or uninstall the game.

Google Play Games Services

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses it for leaderboards and achievements. Google receives your Play Games player profile, your scores and the achievements you earn, and shows your scores on leaderboards to other players. The game tries to sign you in when it starts; if you aren't signed in, it plays normally without leaderboards. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you can choose to watch one to continue a run after losing. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information (such as model, operating system and language)
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalize ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before any ads are requested, and you can change your choice later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Sharing your score

When you tap Share, the game creates an image of your score and opens Android's share menu. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you pick an app to share it with, and the developer does not receive it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for an account, name, email address or phone number.
  • It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.
  • It does not sell your personal information.

Children

Planet Fling is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game's third-party services, contact the developer and it will be addressed.

Security and retention

Progress stays on your device under Android's app protections. Information handled by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
